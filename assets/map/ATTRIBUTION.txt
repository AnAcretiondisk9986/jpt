Schema and style: OpenMapTiles, OpenFreeMap Positron style

The regional vector tiles in tiles/ were cached from
https://tiles.openfreemap.org/planet on 2026-11-25. They cover the trip's
primary regions at zoom levels 4-13. MapLibre GL JS, the style's glyphs and
sprites are bundled with this site. Tiles outside the cached regions and
higher-detail tiles are fetched from OpenFreeMap when online.

BIZ UDPMincho and Noto Sans SC are distributed under the SIL Open Font
License. Their license texts are included in assets/fonts/.
